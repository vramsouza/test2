Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)

  • PLO 1: EL3vate Course Redesign (Frameworks) – Participants will design or redesign an existing course with new content emphasizing frameworks covered within the EL3vate program (i.e., engineering design process and entrepreneurship frameworks).
  • PLO 2: Empower Student-led Problem Solving – Participants will leverage relevant strategies that will empower students to achieve their personal and academic goals while contributing to solving real-world problems as local and global citizens.
  • PLO 3: EL3vate Mindset Shift (Application of the 4Cs) – Participants will engage in the 4Cs and design course activities that highlight the 4Cs (Creativity, Critical Thinking, Collaboration, Communication) in alignment with 21st-century skills.
  • PLO 4: Develop Experiential Learning Opportunities – Participants will devise and implement strategies to build and maintain connections with community partners, seamlessly incorporating these strategies into their course framework. Facilitate interactions with industry professionals or community partners, focusing on customer/partner discovery. This includes place-based problem-solving and service-oriented approaches that align with UH’s Strategic Plan to develop successful students for a better future.
  • PLO 5: Implement Redesigned Course – Participants will implement (i.e., teach) their newly designed course that integrates the frameworks, tools, and concepts introduced during the EL3vate Expedition PD.

How Grades Work

Grade policies, letter grade scale thresholds, and percentage weightings are not explicitly specified in the syllabus. Course completion and evaluation are based on active participation in workshops and completing the primary program deliverables listed below:

Deliverable / Activity Description
Course Map Revisions Developing, drafting, and iteratively refining a redesigned course map incorporating entrepreneurial frameworks, SCAMPER, and EDP wheels.
Miro Board Activities Sharing problem-based activities, value proposition statements, prototype photos, and compassion/listening activities on Miro.
Daily Exit Tickets & Forum Reflections Participating in daily exit tickets (Waterfall Chat, verbal shares, Google Groups Forum reflections).
Final Presentation & RISE Peer Feedback Presenting the redesigned course map on Day 10 and providing constructive feedback to peers on Google Docs using Emily Wray's RISE Model.

Policies

Active Engagement & Mindset

Participants engage in the 4Cs (Creativity, Critical Thinking, Collaboration, Communication) in alignment with 21st-century skills. The program emphasizes an open, collaborative atmosphere to foster growth mindsets and creative problem-solving.

Peer Feedback Framework (RISE Model)

Peer feedback follows Emily Wray's RISE Model. Participants practice active listening and provide structured, meaningful, and constructive feedback during presentations and course map reviews.

Community & Partner Collaboration

Course redesigns are encouraged to integrate place-based problem-solving, service-learning, and active discovery with community partners or industry professionals, aligning with UH's Strategic Plan.
